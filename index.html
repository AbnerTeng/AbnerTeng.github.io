About Me

I’m a first-year Ph.D. student at the Graduate Institute of Network and Multimedia, National Taiwan University (NTU), co-advised by Prof. Pu-Jen Cheng and Prof. Yun-Nung Chen. I am also a Research Scientist at AI Lab, SinoPac Holdings.

Previously, I received my M.S. in Financial Engineering from National Chengchi University (NCCU) in 2024, advised by Prof. Kendro Vincent, and my B.A. in Public Finance from NCCU in 2022. I have also worked at iKala AI Lab and WorldQuant LLC. as a Data Scientist Intern and Research Consultant Intern, respectively.

Research-wise, I’m interested in two main areas:

  1. Generative Retrieval & Recommendation: I investigate continual learning in generative retrieval models and how these models retrieve relevant items while generating responses.

  2. Financial Machine Learning: Beyond conventional training paradigms that minimize prediction error with increasingly complex designs, I explore Learning to Rank (LTR) and simple yet effective models grounded in empirical asset pricing.
