About Me

I’m a first-year Ph.D. student at the Graduate Institute of Network and Multimedia, National Taiwan University (NTU), co-advised by Prof. Pu-Jen Cheng and Prof. Yun-Nung Chen. I am also a Research Scientist at AI Lab, SinoPac Holdings.

Previously, I earned an M.S. in Financial Engineering and a B.A. in Public Finance from National Chengchi University (NCCU), advised by Prof. Kendro Vincent. I have also worked at iKala AI Lab and WorldQuant LLC..

My research focuses on two connected directions:

Generative Retrieval & Recommendation

I investigate continual learning in generative retrieval models and how they retrieve relevant items while generating responses.

Financial Machine Learning

I explore learning to rank and simple, effective models grounded in empirical asset pricing.
